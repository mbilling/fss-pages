SECURE

Locked by default, not by add-on.

TLS 1.3, mTLS and OIDC, deny-by-default ACLs, a hash-chained audit log. Revoking a policy evicts live sessions. Rust with unsafe code forbidden; every parser fuzzed; signed, reproducible builds with SBOM.

RELIABLE

Zero acked messages lost.

Sessions are quorum-replicated by default, so an acknowledged message survives losing a node, even in flight. Grow, shrink and replace nodes with no acked loss. Prometheus, OpenTelemetry, health probes and a Kubernetes operator built in.

CHEAP

Open equals enterprise.

Every feature is Apache-2.0, clustering included, with no paid tier. Durable QoS 1 at 11,900 msg/s per vCPU on 4-vCPU nodes, against HiveMQ 4.18's published 2,812. Fewer machines, no licence: the lowest total cost of ownership is the design goal.

MEASURED, NOT CLAIMED

Durable QoS 1, per vCPU, against HiveMQ.

Every acknowledgement waits for fsync and a second copy on another node: the hardest path a broker serves. Maximum lossless msg/s per vCPU, 2 copies, 200-byte payloads. HiveMQ 4.18's figure is its own published run on 3 × 32 vCPU, the same AMD EPYC Milan family as our CCX23s.

A node's throughput is bound by its one hub task far more than by its vCPU, so small nodes win per vCPU. On HiveMQ's own shape mqttd still carries 1.36× its figure. HiveMQ's number is not reproduced by us and its payload is not published. Sources: the broker's EVIDENCE.md (2026-11-04) and RIG2-RESULTS.md.

Where others win

Mosquitto uses 7× less memory and holds a tighter tail at its own knee. HiveMQ and EMQX have published 100–200M-connection runs; mqttd's runs used 7,200 to 20,000 connections. No other broker has been measured on the current rig yet. And mqttd has no production users.

The plant shape on AWS, v1.3.0, 2026-11-06/07: each site is one bridge connection carrying 250 topics, and one durable $share historian group takes them all. 3–8 nodes are m6i.4xlarge; 12 nodes are c6in.4xlarge, the same CPU behind a network-optimised card, and 2.0M is a floor: the load generators' ceiling. The 5-, 8- and 12-node clusters were grown under load; the 8 → 12 join lost 11 durable sessions, a defect not yet fixed (#1218). With thousands of small publishers instead, durable throughput per node falls from 47.6k to 29.2k between 3 and 10 four-vCPU nodes; that is being profiled.

    HOW IT RUNS

    Pull the plug on a node.

    Devices publish into a masterless mqttd cluster. Every acknowledged message is already replicated before the ack goes out, so a dead node moves its sessions to the survivors and nothing acknowledged is lost.
